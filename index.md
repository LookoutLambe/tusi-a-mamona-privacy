# Privacy Policy — O le Tusi a Mamona Interlinear (Samoan Book of Mormon)

_Last updated: August 9, 2026_

## Summary

**This app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.**

## What we don't collect

- **No accounts or sign-ins.** The app has no login or registration.
- **No analytics or tracking.** We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase
  Analytics, Apple Analytics, or any third-party SDK that monitors how you
  use the app.
- **No advertising.** The app does not contain ads and does not share data
  with advertising networks.
- **No network calls.** All scripture text, English glosses, cross-reference
  data, the app's reading interface, and your reading preferences are loaded
  from files included with the app. No data is transmitted to a server.

## What stays on your device

The following data is stored **only on your device** and is never transmitted
to us or to any third party:

- Highlights you create on verses
- Notes you write on verses
- Font-size and display-mode preferences
- The chapter you were last reading

This data is stored using Apple's standard local storage (`UserDefaults`) and
remains on your device until you delete the app.

## Children

The app does not collect any data, so it does not collect data from children
under 13 either. Parents should always supervise their children's device use.

## Changes

If we ever change how this app handles data, we'll update this page with a new
"Last updated" date. The version of this policy that applies is the one
displayed on this page at the time you use the app.
